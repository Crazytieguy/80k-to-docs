## Summary
- In this role, you'll facilitate weekly peer support groups to help effective altruism community members improve mental resilience and productivity.
- Complete the facilitator training program and study your group's learning materials before sessions begin.
- Facilitate online group sessions weekly for 4-6 participants, supporting evidence-based discussions and peer learning.
- Prepare for each session by reviewing materials, practicing exercises, and submitting feedback.
- Support your peer group between sessions via the online community by sharing learnings and reflections.
