# Fulbright U.S. Scholar Program

**[US Government, The Fulbright Program](https://eca.state.gov/fulbright/fulbright-programs)**

- **Location:** Remote, Global
- **Role type:** Funding
- **Experience:** Multiple experience levels
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less

## Tags
Career development, Research

## Summary
- In this program, you'll conduct teaching, research, or professional projects with a host institution abroad as a Fulbright U.S. Scholar.
- Teach at undergraduate and graduate levels, deliver guest lectures, and lead workshops.
- Conduct scientific, qualitative, or practice-based research to advance knowledge in your field.
- Pursue professional projects including consultations, artist residencies, mentoring, and public engagement.
- Engage with host institution colleagues to build networks and promote international mutual understanding.
