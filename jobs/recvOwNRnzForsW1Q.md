## Summary
- Funding provided for research addressing taste and price barriers preventing mainstream adoption of alternative proteins.
- Address one of four priority areas: off-flavor reduction in plant proteins, fat alternatives for flavor generation, egg replacement, or fish flavor characterization.
- Form interdisciplinary teams combining expertise in analytical chemistry, sensory science, food science, fermentation, breeding, and industry scale-up.
- Establish credible pathways to industry adoption and commercialisation of research outcomes.
- Complete research within 2–3 years with awards ranging from $100,000 to $1 million.
