## Summary
- In this role, you'll build the outcomes-data pipeline measuring fellow and mentor impact to inform MATS' scaling and hiring decisions.
- Design cleaning and verification workflows for automatically collected and self-reported outcome data.
- Consolidate historical data and analyze outcomes to determine fellows' and mentors' marginal impact.

- Coordinate contributors to ensure the pipeline runs reliably and consistently.
