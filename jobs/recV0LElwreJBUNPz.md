# Pre-Doctoral Fellows Program

**[Yale University, Tobin Center for Economic Policy](https://economics.yale.edu/tobin-center)**

- **Location:** New Haven, CT, USA, USA (Confirmed Visas)
- **Role type:** Full-time
- **Experience:** Entry-level
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less

## Tags
Career development: Policy & government, Research, Policy

## Summary
- The Pre-Doctoral Fellows Program at Yale University provides a training experience for individuals considering a Ph.D. in economics or a related discipline.
- Work as full-time research assistants for faculty mentors for one to two years.
- Take or audit one course per semester and participate in a weekly professional development seminar.
- Attend department research seminars and work in common workspaces to encourage camaraderie and peer learning.
- Engage with a diverse community and encourage historically underrepresented groups to apply.
