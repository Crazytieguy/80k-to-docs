## Summary
- This course teaches participants about Safe Pareto Improvements (SPIs), a robust intervention strategy to mitigate risks from AI conflict.
- Covers game theory foundations, bargaining problems, and canonical SPI examples including surrogate goals and delegated game-playing.
- Provides weekly readings, exercises, Slack discussions, and office hours over four weeks with 5-7 hours weekly commitment.
- Offers optional paid capstone projects and two final-week curriculum streams: conceptual or empirical research focus.
