## Summary
- The aim of this RFP is to generate the empirical evidence needed to resolve existing safety questions to inform the potential wider deployment of far-UVC.
- Investigate far-UVC safety across three priority groups with 10 technical areas: General Characterisation, Eye Safety, and Skin Safety.
- Address human exposure, biomarkers, dosimetry, eye and skin safety, and photosensitising medications.
- Submit an Expression of Interest up to 4 pages with proposed approach, study design, timeline, and budget.
