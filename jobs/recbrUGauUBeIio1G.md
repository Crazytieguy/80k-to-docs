## Summary
- In this role, you'll manage the logistics backbone of the lab, ensuring supplies are stocked, inventory is organized, and hazardous waste is handled safely.
- Receive, inspect, and log incoming packages, matching them against purchase orders and flagging discrepancies.
- Manage inventory levels, place and track orders, and coordinate with vendors on ordering and account issues.
- Keep lab spaces, benches, and storage areas clean, organized, stocked, and ready for use.
- Handle hazardous waste per protocol and support basic reagent preparation and process improvements.
