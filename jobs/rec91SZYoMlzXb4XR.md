## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead the development and commissioning of research and policy projects to build advocacy arguments for ending malaria globally and in the UK.
- Generate evidence and insights through analysis of existing data and primary quantitative and qualitative research to drive advocacy campaigns and media coverage.
- Write compelling reports, briefings and inquiry submissions for politicians and policymakers, and support the development of campaign communications.
- Work closely with the media team to develop content for news coverage supporting malaria advocacy goals.
