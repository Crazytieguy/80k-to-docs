## Summary
- In this role, you'll support LEEP's paint program by engaging manufacturers on lead paint elimination in Vietnam.
- Research manufacturers and market dynamics to identify effective engagement routes and key decision-makers.
- Establish relationships and persuade manufacturers to transition to lead-free paint formulations.
- Organize industry workshops and attend government meetings to advance lead paint phaseout discussions.
- Provide translation services, maintain stakeholder lists, and track engagement progress.
