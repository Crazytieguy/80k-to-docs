## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead Lafiya's expansion to a new Sub-Saharan African country by adapting and launching the established model.
- Embed in Nigeria for 3 months to understand the core model and government partnerships.
- Conduct health landscape research and negotiate with Ministries of Health to secure pilot approval.
- Lead the pilot launch by building the operational team, training health workers, and managing evaluation.
- Develop and test new model iterations in year 2, including blended training and additional health interventions.
