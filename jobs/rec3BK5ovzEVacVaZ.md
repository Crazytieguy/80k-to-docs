## Summary
- Funding supports various activities, including graduate study, unpaid internships, and independent study related to mitigating AI or biological risks.
- Open to individuals at any career stage who aim to pursue or transition into careers that could reduce global catastrophic risks.
- Applications are assessed on a rolling basis, with reviews typically within six weeks, expedited decisions possible upon request.
- No fixed maximum or minimum number of applications to be funded; decisions are based on the promise and need for funding.
- Applicants should describe how the funded activity will help transition into impactful careers, and funding may cover cases where sufficient alternative support is lacking.
