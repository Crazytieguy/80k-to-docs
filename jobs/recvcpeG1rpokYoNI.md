## Summary
- In this role, you'll identify novel regulatory frameworks and develop evidence-based solutions for emerging biotechnology products.
- Participate in expert seminars and conduct horizon-scanning to identify biotech products needing regulatory pathways.
- Collaborate with a paired fellow of complementary expertise on multidisciplinary capstone research projects.
- Work with mentors from USDA, EPA, and FDA to shape and refine project approaches.
- Present final capstone project results to agency staff and the Biotechnology Regulatory Fellowship Advisory Committee.
- Our take on this role: We are excited for work in particular on regulation of biotechnology products that increase the risk of [catastrophic pandemics](https://80000hours.org/career-reviews/pandemic-prevention-biosecurity/).
