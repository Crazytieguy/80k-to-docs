## Summary
- In this role, you'll work on projects in the areas of education, agriculture, health, and vaccinations supported by the Development Innovation Lab.
- Assist researchers with literature reviews and systematic searches, drafting parts of such reviews.
- Clean data and help with preliminary analysis essential for research tasks.
- Write summaries of research projects and contribute to the drafting of project reports and policy memos.
- Support day-to-day administrative tasks, including transcribing and note-taking for key meetings with partner organisations.
