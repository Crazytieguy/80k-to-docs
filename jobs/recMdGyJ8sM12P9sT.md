## Summary
- In this fellowship, you'll engage in a mentored learning experience focused on the scientific, translational, and regulatory processes involved in advancing medical countermeasures, including vaccines, therapeutics, diagnostics, and platform technologies.
- Participate in research activities involving scientific literature evaluation, technology landscape analysis, assessment of emerging threats, and review of nonclinical and clinical evidence used to inform medical countermeasure development strategies and preparedness efforts.
