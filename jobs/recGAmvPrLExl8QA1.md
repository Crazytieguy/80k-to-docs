# Grants and Awards Manager

**[Pure Earth](https://www.pureearth.org/)**

- **Location:** New York, NY, USA
- **Role type:** Full-time
- **Experience:** Mid (5-9 years experience)
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less
- **Salary:** $80,000 – $96,900

## Tags
Global health & development, Operations

## Summary
- In this role, you'll manage the complete lifecycle of complex multi-year awards and grants, ensuring compliance and timely reporting.
- Monitor award agreements for donor regulation compliance and manage payments, timelines, and deliverables.
- Prepare narrative and financial reports for donors and coordinate with program teams to collect data and outcomes.
- Train program staff on compliance standards and streamline award management systems.
- Support donor stewardship activities including site visits and facilitate cross-departmental coordination.
