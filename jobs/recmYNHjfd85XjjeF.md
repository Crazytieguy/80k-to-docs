**[Giving What We Can](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/)**

- **Location:** Remote, Global, Various, Global
- **Role type:** Full-time
- **Experience:** Multiple experience levels
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less
- **Location type:** Remote

## Tags
Building effective altruism, Outreach, Operations, Legal

## Summary
- Giving What We Can is looking to help (initially volunteer-led) founding teams organise to grow effective giving in more locations around the world.
- Formulate the strategy, take initial steps, and secure funding to hire an executive director if needed.
- Develop fundraising organisations that can host funds and make grants to effective charities and other high-impact funding opportunities.
- Ensure these organisations can accept donations (ideally tax-deductible) in their jurisdiction and make grants to charitable programs.
- Receive support, including general advice, best practices, and access to shared resources within the effective giving community.
