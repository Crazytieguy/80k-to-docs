## Summary
- In this 6-week online career planning programme, you'll clarify your path toward high-impact work in biosecurity or global health.
- Learn evidence-based career planning frameworks including cause areas, personal strengths, and career capital.
- Work through a structured process to identify, evaluate, and rank your career options.
- Engage with small group sessions, visiting speakers, and individual mentoring from facilitators.
- Receive ongoing support including 1:1 mentoring calls and group follow-ups at 3 and 6 months.
