- **Role type:** Fellowship
- **Experience:** Multiple experience levels
- **Degree:** Doctoral degree

## Tags

## Summary
- In this role, you'll develop and manage cutting-edge research topics under DARPA's Advanced Research Concepts initiative as a two-year Innovation Fellow.
- Collaborate with program managers and research performers to push technological boundaries and explore promising new ideas.
- Evaluate high-risk/high-reward questions and assess potential impacts of further investment.
- Build connections across DARPA's technology-focused network of universities, commercial firms, and non-profit R&D organizations.
- Identify critical technological barriers and craft precise research questions.
