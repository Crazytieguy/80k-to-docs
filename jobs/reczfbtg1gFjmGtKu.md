## Summary
- In this role, you will support virology research efforts under the direction of the lead virologist.
- Perform high-throughput Viral Microneutralization and Hemagglutination Inhibition (HAI) assays to assess anti-influenza antibody efficacy.
- Grow, maintain, and characterize viral and pseudoviral stocks, including influenza and other priority pathogens.
- Perform mammalian cell culture and transfection experiments.
- Assist in viral plaque assays, TCID50 assays, and other virological techniques.
