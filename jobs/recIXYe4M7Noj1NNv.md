## Summary
- In this role, the AI Policy Officer will support supervisory work and will monitor developments relevant to compliance of providers of generative AI models with the EU AI Act.
- Help assess compliance with the decisions of the European Commission.
- Monitor positions and reactions of industry, stakeholders, and media to implementation and enforcement decisions.
- Track technological developments relevant to effective EU AI Act implementation and enforcement.
- Compile feedback from civil society organisations, academia, and companies about practices of AI providers.
