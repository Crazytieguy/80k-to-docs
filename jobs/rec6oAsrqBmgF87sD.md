**[The Broad Institute, Sabeti Lab](https://www.sabetilab.org/)**

- **Location:** Boston metro area, USA

- **Experience:** Junior (1-4 years experience)
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less
- **Salary:** $58,000 – $80,667

## Summary
- In this role, you'll implement and continuously improve pathogen-agnostic metagenomic sequencing workflows to support viral genomics research and pathogen surveillance.
- Execute and troubleshoot complex molecular biology protocols across diverse biospecimens.
- Develop and scale laboratory automation by transitioning manual protocols to automated liquid handling platforms.
