## Summary
- In this role, you'll execute molecular workflows to detect pathogens from environmental samples for Claryx's biosurveillance platform.
- Process environmental samples and perform nucleic-acid extraction with strict quality control protocols.
- Execute multiplex qPCR/RT-qPCR assays for detecting priority pathogens and antimicrobial resistance.
- Prepare metagenomic libraries with targeted enrichment and run Illumina sequencing.
- Maintain BSL-2 laboratory standards and track inventories while contributing to SOP improvements.
