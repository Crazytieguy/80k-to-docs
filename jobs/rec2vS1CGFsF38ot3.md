# No.10 Innovation Fellowships

**[UK Government, 10 Downing Street](https://no10innovationfellows.campaign.gov.uk/)**

- **Location:** London, UK, Various, UK, UK
- **Role type:** Fellowship
- **Experience:** Multiple experience levels
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less
- **Salary:** $114,309 – $161,378

## Tags
Career development: Policy & government, Research, Policy

## Summary
- The No.10 Innovation Fellowship offers a 6-12 month posting with the possibility of extending it.
- Fellows work on priority issues to the Prime Minister, tackling them with technical skills and fresh thinking.
- Areas of focus include AI applications and digital solutions to public problems.
- Responsibilities include leading high-impact technology and data projects and possibly mentoring others.
- Fellowship includes access to senior decision-makers and mentorship from the No.10 Data Science team.
