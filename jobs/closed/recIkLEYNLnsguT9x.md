# Senior Program Officer

**GiveWell**

**Areas & skills:** Global health & development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead a grantmaking portfolio, making strategic decisions about which opportunities to fund based on rigorous analysis and evidence.
- Develop funding strategies by combining evidence review, cost-effectiveness modeling, and ground-truthing to identify high-impact programs.
- Evaluate grantees' capacity and effectiveness to ensure on-the-ground delivery matches reported outputs.
- Communicate research findings externally to donors and mentor team members on grantmaking approaches.
- Collaborate with Senior Researchers to incorporate analysis into grantmaking decisions.
