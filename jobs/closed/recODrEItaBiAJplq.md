# Science-Policy Visiting Fellow, Emerging Technology and Verification Processes

**United Nations University**

**Areas & skills:** Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness

## Summary
- In this role, you'll research how emerging technologies could enhance compliance and verification for the Biological Weapons Convention.
- Develop analytical reports assessing AI, synthetic biology, and advanced sensing applications to BWC verification functions.
- Engage with disarmament actors through briefings and presentations on strengthening the Convention.
- Organize workshops bringing together scientific experts, diplomats, and civil society to refine research findings.
- Applicants must be from the global south.
