# Fellowship

**Impact First**

**Areas & skills:** Building effective altruism

## Summary
- In this role, you'll understand the world's pressing problems and identify where you can contribute most effectively.
- Explore curated readings and exercises for 2-4 hours weekly to build knowledge on high-impact global challenges.
- Attend weekly 90-minute facilitated discussions with peers to examine cause areas and ethical foundations.
- Participate in a two-day kick-off weekend to connect with your cohort and engage with hard questions.
- Work one-on-one to translate your insights into actionable next steps and develop an impact-focused plan.
