# Principal Expert / Group Leader, Emergency Preparedness and Response

**European Union, Centre for Disease Prevention and Control**

**Areas & skills:** Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness

## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead ECDC's emergency preparedness and response group, providing expert guidance on public health crisis management.
- Coordinate with member states and international partners on emergency response strategies and protocols.
- Develop and maintain frameworks for preparedness planning and rapid response activation.

- Provide scientific and technical advice to support strategic emergency decision-making and crisis management.
