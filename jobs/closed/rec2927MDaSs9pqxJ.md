# Nuclear Policy Certificate Program (2026)

**Nonproliferation Policy Education Center**

**Areas & skills:** Nuclear security

## Summary
- This course provides technical, legal, and historical background on civil and military nuclear policy, weapons design, and nonproliferation.
- Includes 12 formal lectures and 11 guest lectures covering nuclear deterrence, arms control, terrorism, economics, and proliferation intelligence.
- Features expert instruction from the former Deputy for Nonproliferation Policy at the Department of Defense.
- Concludes with a war game applying knowledge to contemporary nuclear policy challenges.
- Student certificate offered alongside a certificate for government employees and service members.
