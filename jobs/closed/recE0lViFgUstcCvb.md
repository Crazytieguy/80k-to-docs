# Bulletin Editorial Fellow

**University of Chicago, Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists**

**Areas & skills:** Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness, Global health & development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll write regular columns on existential threats for the Bulletin's global platform.
- Produce four published articles per year on nuclear risk, climate change, and disruptive technologies.
- Collaborate closely with a designated editor to shape and refine your stories.
- Promote your published work and highlight your Bulletin affiliation in media interviews and outreach.
- Help diversify the Bulletin's contributor base with fresh perspectives on humanity's greatest challenges.
