# Program Associate, Asia

**Lead Exposure Elimination Project**

**Areas & skills:** Global health & development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll support LEEP's paint programs across Asia, coordinating with governments, manufacturers, and civil society partners to advance lead paint elimination.
- Work with governments to introduce and implement lead paint regulations.
- Engage with manufacturers to encourage lead paint phase-out and identify sustainable solutions.
- Conduct paint studies and collect country-specific data to inform policy decisions.
- Prepare advocacy communications and provide technical support to key stakeholders.
