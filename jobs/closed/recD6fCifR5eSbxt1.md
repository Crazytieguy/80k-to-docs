# Research Assistant, Malaria Vaccine

**Oxford University, Nuffield Department of Population Health**

**Areas & skills:** Global health & development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll perform immunological assays to measure vaccine responses in malaria vaccine development trials.
- Conduct bead-based multiplex serological assays, ELISA, ELISPOT, and sterile cell culture on human blood samples.
- Process samples, prepare reagents, maintain supplies, and support the immunology programme.
- Catalogue frozen samples, maintain inventories, and perform data analysis.
- Work to GCP and HTA standards with attention to detail and accuracy.
