# Externship / Student Volunteer, Transparency and Regulatory Affairs (Fall 2026 and Spring 2027)

**Animal Legal Defense Fund**

**Areas & skills:** Animal welfare

## Summary
- In this externship, you'll support the Transparency and Regulatory Affairs Program through legal research and regulatory advocacy to protect animals.
- Conduct research and draft memoranda on regulatory matters affecting exploited animals.
- Assist in drafting regulatory comments, petitions for rulemaking, FOIA requests, and appeals.
- Help gather data and create spreadsheets tracking animal-exploiting industries.
- Draft regulatory complaints and support factual research for the program's advocacy work.
