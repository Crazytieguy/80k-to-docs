# Careers Fellowship

**Jewish Social Impact Network**

**Areas & skills:** Building effective altruism

## Summary
- In this 8-week cohort-based fellowship, you'll align your career with Jewish values and evidence-based impact.
- Meet weekly with a small cohort and experienced facilitator to discuss and wrestle with career decisions.
- Work through readings and exercises exploring Jewish sources on obligation and frameworks for identifying high-impact work.
- Assess your own strengths, constraints, and options to develop an impact-oriented career strategy.
- Create a concrete career plan with a specific next step tailored to your current circumstances and goals.
