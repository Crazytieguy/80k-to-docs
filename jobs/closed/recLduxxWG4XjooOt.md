# Senior Programme Manager, Public Health Emergencies and Pandemics

**Gavi**

**Areas & skills:** Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness

## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead Gavi's preparedness and rapid response to vaccine-preventable public health emergencies and pandemics.
- Triage emerging health threats and maintain information sharing agreements with key partners including WHO and UNICEF.
- Lead rapid assessments and recommendations to inform funding and operational response decisions.

- Drive operational delivery of emergency responses including approvals, disbursements, and implementation support.
