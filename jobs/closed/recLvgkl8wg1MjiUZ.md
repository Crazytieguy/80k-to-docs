# Impact Cohort Manager

**Effective Altruism Switzerland**

**Areas & skills:** Building effective altruism

## Summary
- In this role, you'll own the Impact Cohort, a career-advising program helping altruistically-minded people in Switzerland pursue high-impact careers.
- Deliver personalized 1-on-1 career advising across both program phases, supporting participants on applications and career progress.
- Actively promote the cohort to reach new audiences and build a larger participant pipeline.
- Lead the Impact Community by running community events and maintaining relationships with participants and alumni.
- Collect feedback and track outcomes to measure impact and use data to refine and expand the program.
