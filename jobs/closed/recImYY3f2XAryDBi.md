# Policy Fellow, Global Innovation

**Information Technology and Innovation Foundation**

**Areas & skills:** Career development: Policy & government

## Summary
- In this role, you'll conduct research on global innovation and trade policy, supporting the Global Trade and Innovation Policy Alliance.
- Maintain and update the GTIPA website and produce monthly newsletters.
- Research countries' innovation and trade policies affecting advanced-technology sectors including semiconductors, AI, and biotechnology.
- Identify and track global trade barriers related to digital trade and data flows.
- Write policy articles and op-eds, and provide research support such as literature reviews.
