# PhD Research Fellowship, Safety and Security Evaluation of Deployed AI Systems

**Simula**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll develop methods and tools for evaluating, red-teaming, and monitoring deployed AI systems.
- Extend open-source toolsets (SimpleAudit, swarm-attack, Moltbook Observatory) to strengthen capabilities.
- Research failure patterns to explain why and when systems fail in real-world deployments.
- Collaborate with institutional partners in healthcare and public administration contexts.
- Create vendor-independent tools for regulators and auditors to independently evaluate systems.
