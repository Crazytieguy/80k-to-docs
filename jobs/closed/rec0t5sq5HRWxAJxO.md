# Research Associate, Infectious Disease Modelling

**Imperial College London, School of Public Health**

**Areas & skills:** Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness

## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead Imperial's contribution to an international research project on mathematical modelling of within-host immune systems for pandemic preparedness.
- Develop mathematical models to evaluate immunological assays for viral surveillance and spillover detection.
- Collaborate with international teams including Oxford, Institut Pasteur, NUS and A*STAR on emerging infectious disease research.
- Synthesise scientific data to assess novel immunological tools for broader pandemic surveillance strategies.
- Travel to the region as required to support international collaboration.
