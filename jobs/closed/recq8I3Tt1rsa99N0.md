# Cyber Policy and Strategy Planner

**Government of California, Office of Emergency Services**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll support California's AI Safety Reporting Program by designing and refining AI safety policies, analysing incident reports, and preparing legislative reports.
- Analyse AI safety incident submissions to identify risks and patterns.
- Compile and interpret incident data for annual reporting and continuous improvement of statewide processes.
- Coordinate with Cal-CSIC, California Department of Technology, and partner agencies to advance AI safety initiatives.
- Communicate policy implications and technical findings clearly to executives and stakeholders.
- For application support, contact implementation@secureaiproject.org.
