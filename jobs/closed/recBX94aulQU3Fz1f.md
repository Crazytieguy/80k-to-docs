# Technical Remediation Manager

**Pure Earth**

**Areas & skills:** Global health & development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll provide technical guidance on contaminated site assessment and risk mitigation within Pure Earth's Global Lead Program.
- Develop protocols, guidance documents, and country-specific remediation guidelines for environmental interventions.
- Deliver technical support and field training on environmental sampling, XRF usage, and site remediation alternatives.
- Support collection, analysis, and use of environmental data to inform intervention designs and evaluate impact.
- Contribute to research publications and represent Pure Earth at key stakeholder events.
