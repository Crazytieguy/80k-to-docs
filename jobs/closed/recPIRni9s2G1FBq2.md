# Program Officer

**Arthropoda Foundation**

**Areas & skills:** Animal welfare

## Summary
- In this role, you'll identify opportunities to improve farmed shrimp welfare and commission research to address them.
- Conduct structured conversations with shrimp farmers, scientists, and industry stakeholders to map welfare issues and scientific gaps.
- Co-design research projects with scientists by identifying priority questions and shaping proposals with clear theories of change.
- Facilitate grantmaking activities including reviewing proposals and managing grantees.
- Travel domestically and internationally to engage with stakeholders and conduct site visits.
