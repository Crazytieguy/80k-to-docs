**OpenAI**

- Train and evaluate large language models using reinforcement learning and empirical methods.
- Develop evaluations for model failure modes including hallucination, instruction-following failures, and scheming.

- Integrate successful alignment techniques into model training and deployment workflows, producing publishable research.
