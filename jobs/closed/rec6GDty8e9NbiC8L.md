# Request for Proposals, Extreme Power Concentration

**Longview Philanthropy**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- Longview Philanthropy seeks to fund proposals to better understand and reduce the risk of AI enabling the extreme concentration of power.
- Funds projects across twelve areas: foundational research, monitoring, AI integrity, company policies, law-following AI, government oversight, autonomous weapons, surveillance, political economy, citizen tools, compute, field building.

- Proposals should target power concentration, respond rapidly, aim ambitiously, and anticipate advancing AI.
