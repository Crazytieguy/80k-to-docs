# Research Scientist, Safety Oversight, London

**Google DeepMind**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll develop systems to monitor deployed AI models for safety issues, detecting misbehavior and misuse.
- Build classifiers and data pipelines to detect model misbehavior and misuse end-to-end.
- Research and develop cross-context monitoring systems to identify coordinated harms using signal aggregation methods.
- Explore novel monitoring methods using model activations, actions, chains-of-thought and outputs.
- Collaborate with infrastructure teams and data scientists to scale solutions and report findings to the safety team.
