# Senior Research Associate, Logic and Programming Languages for AI Safety

**University of Bristol, Department of Computer Science**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll conduct research on logic and programming languages for AI safety, developing formal foundations and methods for reasoning about software and AI systems.
- Design, implement and evaluate software tools that apply formal methods, including agentic programming tools.
- Write research for publication in international venues and present findings at conferences and seminars.
- Collaborate with colleagues in the School of Computer Science and the Safeguarded AI research community.
- Apply rigorous formal reasoning and analytical skills to advance theoretical understanding.
