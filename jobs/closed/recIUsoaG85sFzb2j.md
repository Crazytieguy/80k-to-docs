# Program Assistant, Bangladesh

**Pure Earth**

**Areas & skills:** Global health & development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll support program implementation, reporting, and coordination of activities focused on environmental pollution reduction.
- Draft and support production of reports, presentations, and meeting minutes for program materials.
- Support data collection, GPS mapping, and entry of environmental and toxic site data into databases.
- Coordinate logistics and administration for events, workshops, field visits, and meetings.
- Conduct field visits and desk research on environmental health and pollution topics.
