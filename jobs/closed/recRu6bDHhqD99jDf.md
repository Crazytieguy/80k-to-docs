# Editor in Chief

**University of Chicago, Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists**

**Areas & skills:** Macrostrategy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead editorial coverage of nuclear risk, climate change, and disruptive technologies, managing staff and advancing the mission.
- Establish and uphold editorial standards across all platforms and serve as final authority on published content.
- Consult with the Science and Security Board on existential risks and co-author the Doomsday Clock Statement.
- Build and maintain networks of scientists, policymakers, and writers to strengthen credibility and reach.
- Expand investigative reporting and collaborate on strategy, budget management, and online presence.
