# Investigative Technologist, Consumer Protection Section

**California Department of Justice**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll conduct technical investigations and provide subject matter expertise in consumer protection, privacy, and technology enforcement.
- Apply technical skills in software engineering, data science, and research to complex investigative questions.
- Serve as a subject matter expert on interdisciplinary investigative and enforcement teams.
- Support investigations into violations of consumer protection and privacy laws.
- Contribute technical expertise to legislative, policy, and educational initiatives.
- Our take on this role: Because this office is responsible for enforcing [SB 53](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/what-is-californias-ai-safety-law/), you'll have a rare opportunity to ensure frontier AI developers comply with safety requirements.
