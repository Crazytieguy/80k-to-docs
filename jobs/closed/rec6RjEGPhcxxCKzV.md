# Fellowship, Hague Code of Conduct Youth Group

**Fondation pour la Recherche Stratégique**

**Areas & skills:** Career development: Policy & government

## Summary
- This is an 18-month fellowship programme combining expert engagement with individual research on missile non-proliferation.
- Participate in four thematic webinars with leading researchers and diplomats discussing ballistic missile issues.
- Attend a three-day residential workshop in Lithuania featuring expert briefings and simulation exercises.
- Conduct independent research on a technical or diplomatic theme and submit a paper for the published collection.
- Engage in optional virtual cafés to maintain group cohesion and exchange perspectives on developments.
