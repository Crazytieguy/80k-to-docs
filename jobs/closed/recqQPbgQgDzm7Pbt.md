# Research Fellow, Malaria Vector Control

**University of London, School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine**

**Areas & skills:** Global health & development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll support epidemiological and statistical components of two malaria vector control studies in Côte d'Ivoire and Djibouti.
- Develop sub-protocols, SOPs, ethics applications and data collection tools for both study sites.
- Coordinate epidemiological data collection with team members to ensure high-quality data.
- Conduct statistical analysis and communicate findings to academics, officials and funders.
- Contribute to peer-reviewed publications and grant proposals while supporting junior team members.
