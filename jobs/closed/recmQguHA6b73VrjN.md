# Epidemiology and Information Analyst

**UK Government, Health Security Agency**

**Areas & skills:** Career development

## Summary
- In this role, you'll analyze data to support infectious disease surveillance and outbreak investigations.
- Contribute to routine surveillance reports by adapting routines and performing descriptive analysis.
- Undertake basic statistical tests and use software tools to produce tables, graphs, maps and queries.
- Respond to data requests and support research activities through data analysis.
- Maintain data governance and information security standards in your work.
