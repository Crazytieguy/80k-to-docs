# Research Fellow, Malaria Control Interventions

**University of London, School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine**

- In this role, you'll lead a project developing epidemiological methods to measure malaria control intervention effectiveness.
- Lead the LSHTM component and collaborate with research partners across Guinea, Burkina Faso, and Mali.
- Conduct statistical analyses of routinely collected health data using advanced statistical techniques.
- Apply conditional logistic regression, survival analysis, and multilevel modelling methods.
- Use statistical software such as R and/or Python to develop analytical frameworks.
