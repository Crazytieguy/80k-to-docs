# Policy Officer, Medical Counter Measures Intelligence and Innovation

**European Union, European Commission**

**Areas & skills:** Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness

## Summary
- In this role, you'll strengthen intelligence gathering and support innovation for medical countermeasures to prepare for and respond to health crises.
- Identify early warning signals of health threats and assess medical countermeasures in the market and pipeline.
- Analyse health crisis scenarios using data analysis and artificial intelligence.
- Design interventions to strengthen availability and accessibility of medical countermeasures and diagnostics.
- Research stakeholder information on health threats, innovative products, and innovation support mechanisms.
