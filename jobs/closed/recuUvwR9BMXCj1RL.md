# Research Scientist, Diagnostics

**The Broad Institute, Sabeti Lab**

**Areas & skills:** Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness

## Summary
- In this role, you'll develop and test PCR and CRISPR-based diagnostic assays for infectious disease detection and point-of-care deployment.
- Design and carry out experiments independently and collaboratively to advance diagnostic tools and technologies.
- Troubleshoot experimental challenges and investigate new methods to improve assay performance.
- Maintain comprehensive experimental records, documentation, and written protocols for reproducibility.
- Communicate research progress, findings, and obstacles to the diagnostics team and collaborators.
