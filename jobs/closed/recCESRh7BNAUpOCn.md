# Course, AI Agents and the Law (Summer 2026)

**Vista Institute for AI Policy**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- This course teaches how law and policy apply to increasingly capable AI agents through two modules.
- Module 1 builds foundational knowledge of AI agents, technical concepts, and current governance mechanisms.
- Module 2 trains participants to conduct original research across liability, legal alignment, infrastructure, and regulatory frameworks.
- Participants develop research proposals for an optional writing competition with cash prizes.
