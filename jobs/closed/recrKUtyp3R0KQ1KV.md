# Data Reporting Analyst, Artificial Intelligence Safety Reporting Program

**Government of California, Office of Emergency Services**

**Areas & skills:** AI safety & policy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll support California's AI Safety Reporting Program by analyzing incident submissions and preparing reports for executives and policymakers.
- Review and assess critical AI safety incident reports for accuracy and compliance.
- Compile and interpret data to produce annual reports and evidence-based recommendations.
- Coordinate with multiple state agencies including Cal-CSIC, CDT, and CMD to refine reporting processes.
- Translate complex AI and cybersecurity information into clear communications for executive leadership.
- If you're interested, the Secure AI Project encourages you to reach out to implementation@secureaiproject.org before applying for important tips on the application process.
