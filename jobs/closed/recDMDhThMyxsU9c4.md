# Research and Outreach Officer

**Social Change Lab**

**Areas & skills:** Macrostrategy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll conduct research on social movements and share findings with activists, funders and journalists.
- Lead research projects across climate, animal advocacy and AI safety, selecting methods appropriate to research questions.
- Develop accessible outputs including blog posts, op-eds, reports and media content for non-specialist audiences.
- Conduct media outreach, present at conferences and events, and engage directly with activists and philanthropists.
- Support grant applications, manage research datasets and contribute to team meetings and retreats.
