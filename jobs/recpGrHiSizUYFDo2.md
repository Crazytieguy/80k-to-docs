## Summary
- In this role, you will carry out the office's legislative oversight functions covering a broad range of government programs and activities.
- Coordinate, review, and clear draft legislation, testimony, and formal correspondence from Executive Branch agencies to Congress.
- Evaluate legislative proposals to determine their relationship to the President's policies and legislative priorities and advise policy officials on potential conflicts.
- Analyze, prepare, and coordinate the interagency review and clearance of Statements of Administration Policy.
