# Head of Community

**[Effective Altruism DC](https://effectivealtruismdc.org/)**

- **Location:** Washington, DC metro area, USA
- **Role type:** Full-time
- **Experience:** Junior (1-4 years experience)
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less
- **Salary:** $81,000 – $92,000

## Tags
Building effective altruism, Outreach, Operations

## Summary
- In this role, you'll build EA DC's community, owning programming, events, and infrastructure that help 1,500+ members work on global challenges.
- Conduct 1-on-1 calls and career advising with community members, welcoming new people and providing tailored guidance.
- Plan and run events including cause-area meetups, workshops, happy hours, and occasional retreats.
- Keep the Airtable CRM, website, and community resources current and accurate.
- Manage social media across Facebook, LinkedIn, and Slack.
