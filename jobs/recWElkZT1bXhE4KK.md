## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead forecasting for an assigned vaccine portfolio, producing demand, supply, price, and cost projections for decision-making.
- Develop and maintain vaccine forecasting models to ensure forecasts are transparent, robust, and fit for purpose.
- Coordinate collection, validation, and integration of programme inputs into forecast updates.

- Monitor actuals against forecasts, highlighting risks and issues affecting programme performance and supply continuity.
