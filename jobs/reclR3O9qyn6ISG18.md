## Summary
- In this role, you'll own the end-to-end movement of samples, coordinating vendors and shipping providers to ensure reliable lab delivery.
- Manage cold-chain logistics, inbound scheduling, compliance, and shipping regulations while resolving delivery issues.
- Serve as the operational point of contact for sampling partners, including onboarding and maintaining relationships.
- Build tracking systems, dashboards, and SOPs that scale with growing sampling volume.
- Coordinate with Lab and data teams on chain of custody, metadata, and sample handoffs.
