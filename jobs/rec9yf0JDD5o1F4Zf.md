## Summary
- This is a 6-week online career planning course designed to help doctors and medical students chart high-impact career pathways in global health.
- Identify your personal values and understand impact frameworks aligned with solving pressing health problems.
- Learn career planning tools including career capital assessment and the Weighted Factor Model.
- Refine your career options from a long list to a prioritised short list through structured decision-making.
- Develop actionable next steps with ongoing 1:1 mentoring and peer support.
