# Research Fellow, Infectious Disease Modelling

**[National University of Singapore, Asia Centre for Health Security](https://nus.edu.sg/)**

- **Location:** Singapore, Singapore, Singapore, Asia
- **Role type:** Fellowship
- **Experience:** Junior (1-4 years experience)
- **Degree:** Doctoral degree

## Tags
Biosecurity & pandemic preparedness, Global health & development, Research

## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead research developing within-host mathematical models to understand long-term dynamics of immunity to pathogens.
- Develop mathematical models to test hypotheses on durability of acquired immunity through infection, vaccination or hybrid immunity.
- Collaborate with interdisciplinary teams across Singapore, Vietnam, Cambodia and the UK.
- Analyze data from longitudinal studies tracking immune responses to infection/vaccination.
- Disseminate research findings through peer-reviewed publications and presentations at international conferences.
