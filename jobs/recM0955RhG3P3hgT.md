## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead and support LEEP's paint programs across multiple countries, managing government engagement, industry assistance, and research initiatives.
- Work with governments and policy-makers to introduce and enforce lead paint regulations.
- Engage with paint manufacturers to support the transition to lead-free products and provide technical assistance.
- Conduct paint studies to gather country-specific data on lead paint exposure.
- Design project plans, troubleshoot program challenges, and recruit local partners to achieve LEEP's goals.
