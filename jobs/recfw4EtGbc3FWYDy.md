# Program Management Consultant, Rwanda

**[Pure Earth](https://www.pureearth.org/)**

- **Location:** Africa, Rwanda
- **Role type:** Full-time
- **Experience:** Mid (5-9 years experience)
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less

## Tags
Global health & development, Outreach, Strategy

## Summary
- In this role, you'll support stakeholder engagement and project scoping for Pure Earth's lead poisoning prevention work in Rwanda.
- Identify and build relationships with key stakeholders across government, academia, and NGO sectors.
- Conduct a desk review of Rwanda's "lead landscape," analyzing health impacts and existing policies.
- Research operational requirements for Pure Earth to establish presence in Rwanda.
- Develop a project work plan aligned with local context and organizational mission.
