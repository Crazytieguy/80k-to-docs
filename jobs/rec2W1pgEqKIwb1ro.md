**[Sinergia Animal](https://www.sinergiaanimal.org/)**

- **Location:** Bogota, Colombia, Colombia, South America
- **Role type:** Full-time
- **Experience:** Junior (1-4 years experience)
- **Degree:** Undergraduate degree or less

## Tags
Animal welfare, Outreach, Operations

## Summary
- In this role, you'll lead a program to reduce animal product consumption in Colombian institutions by implementing plant-based eating days.
- Build relationships with decision-makers in schools, universities, and government to advocate for meat reduction initiatives.
- Develop strategic plans and give lectures to help institutions implement plant-based eating days.
- Coordinate with chefs, dietitians, and health experts to ensure successful program implementation.
- Represent Sinergia Animal at public events and respond to media requests to promote plant-based initiatives.
